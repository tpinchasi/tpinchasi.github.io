What IFocus is

IFocus is a free Android app that summarizes your calls and messages with the people you choose, and reminds you of what they asked of you. It works on your phone. The app is still in development and testing, and is provided as is.

Your part

  • Use the app only on your own phone and accounts, and within the law where you are, including the laws on recording calls and on reading messages. Calls are recorded by your phone's own Phone app, not by IFocus.
  • Respect the privacy of the people whose calls and messages the app summarizes, and don't share the summaries without their consent.
  • Sign up only in sheets you're allowed to edit, and check what was written.
  • Linking WhatsApp as a linked device is an unofficial connection: WhatsApp may restrict it, and you use it at your own risk.

Summaries and reminders can be wrong

Transcripts, summaries, reminders and events are made automatically, by AI models, and can be incomplete or wrong. Don't rely on them for anything important (health, money, legal matters, safety) without checking the source.
